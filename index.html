bioRxiv Sweeper

Sweep the most recent bioRxiv & medRxiv preprints for the subject areas you care about and get back a single, readable three-level digest — total synthesis, by-topic, and by-paper — with hyperlinked APA citations. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere except the preprint API and your chosen LLM.

Stored only in this browser (localStorage) and sent directly to Anthropic. Get one at console.anthropic.com.
